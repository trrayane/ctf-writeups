# Microvault — Terms of Service (Internal)
# Effective: 2026-Q4

These terms govern use of Microvault by Microclub staff. Use of the
service implies acceptance.

## 1. Permitted use

Microvault may be used to store internal-only material that is not
classified above "Internal Only". It may NOT be used to store:

  - Personally-identifying information beyond Microclub member rosters.
  - Customer or sponsor data.
  - Material under embargo or NDA.
  - Material classified "Confidential" or above.
  - Cryptographic key material.

If you are uncertain whether a document may be stored, ask the
DevOps team before uploading.

## 2. Encryption disclaimer

Microvault encrypts files at rest using an in-house primitive
maintained by the cryptography team. The primitive is NOT a vetted
cryptographic construction and NOT cleared for material above
"Internal Only". The encryption is provided as a defence in depth
against casual disk-level access, not as a strong confidentiality
guarantee.

## 3. Access

Access to Microvault is controlled at the network layer. Production
deployments are intended to be reachable only from the Microclub
internal network. The MCP debug interface is intended for engineering
diagnostics only.

If you can read this document, you have already authenticated to the
internal network. Do not redistribute the access URL.

## 4. Audit

All read operations against the MCP interface are logged. Logs are
retained for 90 days and reviewed weekly by the audit team. Anomalous
access patterns may result in revocation of access.

## 5. Liability

Microvault is provided AS-IS for internal use. The Microclub DevOps
team makes no warranty of fitness for any particular purpose. In
particular, do not rely on Microvault availability for anything
operationally critical.

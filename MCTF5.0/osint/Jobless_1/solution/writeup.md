# Writeup

### Instagram:

The solver should first search for stay_the_jobless in social media, and they'll find an acc on instagram.

Then, they check his followers, and find "kamal_lbettal". They should notice that his profile picture is discord's logo.

### Discord:

After that, the solver would add a new friend in discord with the same username as the instagram account. They can check his profile by viewing the pending requests, and there they'll find a link to an unknown channel.

The link can be copied and then analyzed. The format of a discord channel link is: "discord.com/channels/serverid/channelid", and if the widgets are on in the server, anyone can get an invite.

There are tools that do that, otherwise the solver can simple just craft it manually: https://discord.com/widget?id="serverid".

In the server, a conversation between "Kamal_lbettal" and his friend can be found. There, they'll find a screenshot of a deleted post from stay, featuring a picture with a foreigner he randomly met.

The link of the post is visible from the screenshot.

### Deleted Post:

The first intuition is to use wayback machine, but instagram posts can't really be found there that easily. By searching for an alternative to it that actually saves instagram pages, they can find "Archive.today".

After finding the page, initially there isn't anything more than what appears on the screenshot. But in the caption, stay mentions that the guy he met "Andrei" is blind. So this can be taken as a hint to check the alt text (can be viewed in the source of the page).

There, they'll find the full name of the foreigner.

### Andrei:

By searching his full name in Facebook, they'll find an account.

They can notice that he was tagged in a post by another account, which is a new account of stay.\*

In the comments, they'll find a conversation between the two, where Andrei mentions that he lent a book to family member, who dropped it in the middle of the ocean, in addition to a picture inside a plane, with an apparant flight number and a time.

Stay mentions that he found the book, and then shares some interesting details, which can be used as a hint.

### The book:

The solver should track the flight in the picture, and pinpoint the exact location of the plane at the time shown.

By checking the place on google maps, they won't find anything. But just next to it, there's a viewpoint.

By searching more about the coordinates of that location, and using the hint stay mentions in the comment, they should find a reddit post explaining that the location is some sort of null-island, and there are thousands of businesses listed nearby.

In the comments of the reddit post, the most recent one mentions that they found a book in the middle of the ocean near that location, and they share the link of the listing.

The flag can be found in that listing.
